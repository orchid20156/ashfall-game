ASHFALL

You begin in the Sanctuary — a golden warded circle at the world's heart where monsters can never attack you. Walk past the glowing boundary (marked by the "EXIT" gate) whenever you're ready to fight, and walk back in anytime to heal up safely. A Wandering Trader waits inside the Sanctuary — walk up and press E to buy stronger gear, runes, and potions with gold, or sell items you don't need. Monsters sometimes drop Health Potions (+150 HP) and Mana Potions (+75 MP) — press Q or R to drink one instantly, or click one in your Inventory. Survive to wave 3 to earn a Spectral Ally that auto-attacks nearby monsters for you — it has 300 HP and can be hurt, but heals fully whenever you return to a sanctuary. Ctrl+Click a weapon or armor in your inventory to equip it to your companion and boost its damage and armor. The Ashlands stretch far beyond your screen — explore freely and watch the minimap in the corner. Move with WASD, attack with the mouse click facing your cursor, or press Enter to auto-attack the nearest enemy without aiming. Earn skill points and stat points as you level. Each class has its own exclusive skill path (Warrior, Archer, or Wizard) with three skills gated by character level (1/5/10) — unlock them into your hotbar (keys 1–6); skills auto-aim at your nearest enemy when used, no cursor aiming needed. Then spend further skill points to upgrade them with more damage, faster casting, or lower mana cost (down to a minimum of 2). Gear drops from fallen foes — walk over it to collect within 20 seconds, or it despawns. Monsters also drop gold (goblins 3-5, slimes 10, skeletons 35, orcs 50, bosses 100). Some armor has sockets — six runes (A–F) drop from monsters and grant bonus Strength, Vitality, Intellect, Dexterity, Health, or Mana. Goblins (red) drop runes rarely, slimes (green) more often, and skeletons/orcs/bosses (white) most often. Higher runes need a higher character level to socket, but you can also craft them: Alt+Click two matching runes in your inventory to fuse them into one rune of the next tier (2 A→B, 2 B→C, 2 C→D, 2 D→E, 2 E→F). Socketing the right rune sequence forms powerful runewords — hover a socketed armor piece to see the possibilities, from single-rune resonances up to the mighty 3-rune Trinity, Ascension, and Titan's Wrath. At wave 20 you'll face the Demon King — 500 HP, and every 5 seconds he unleashes a ring of 30 fireballs (50 damage each) in all directions, dodges exactly every 10th hit you land on him, and drops a guaranteed Ultimate Weapon (30-40 damage, and it never despawns) if you defeat him — doing so also tears open a path to a Frozen Realm beyond the ashlands, complete with its own sanctuary and monsters (Ice Demons, Beasts, Skeletons, Giants, and Golems, all with a chance to freeze you solid). At wave 20 in the ice you'll face the King of the Icelands — 750 HP, freezing rings of ice bolts every 25 seconds — and defeating him opens the way to the Land of Ruins, where Alexander the Mighty King awaits at wave 20: 1250 HP, a ring of 75 swords every 15 seconds dealing 100 damage and a 10-second freeze that keeps burning you each second it lasts. Defeating Alexander opens the way to the Desert Lands — a scorching wasteland with its own sanctuary and monsters (Desert Zombies, Intruders, Skeletons, and Beasts). At wave 20 there you'll face the true final boss, the King of the Scorching Sun — 2000 HP, unleashing a ring of 65 fireballs every 20 seconds dealing 300 damage each. Defeat him for the ultimate reward and to complete your legend. Your progress saves automatically as you play, so you can close the game anytime and continue later. Press ~ anytime in-game to open Creative Mode for testing — god mode, free XP/points, item and rune spawning, and wave control.
